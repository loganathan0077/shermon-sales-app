# Installing Sales App as a Mobile App 📱

Your Sales App is now a **Progressive Web App (PWA)**! This means you can install it on your phone like a native app.

## Benefits of Installing as PWA

✅ **Works like a native app** - Appears on your home screen with an icon
✅ **Faster loading** - Cached for quick access
✅ **Offline support** - Works even without internet (limited)
✅ **Full-screen experience** - No browser bars
✅ **Camera access** - Barcode scanner works properly on mobile
✅ **Push notifications** - (Can be added later if needed)

## How to Install on Android

### Method 1: Chrome Browser (Recommended)

1. **Open Chrome** on your Android phone
2. **Visit:** https://sales-app.web.app
3. **Look for the install prompt** at the bottom of the screen
   - You'll see: "Add Sales App to Home screen"
4. **Tap "Install"** or "Add to Home screen"
5. **Done!** The app icon will appear on your home screen

### Method 2: Manual Installation

If you don't see the automatic prompt:

1. **Open Chrome** and visit https://sales-app.web.app
2. **Tap the 3-dot menu** (⋮) in the top right
3. **Select "Add to Home screen"** or "Install app"
4. **Name it** "Sales App" (or keep the default)
5. **Tap "Add"**
6. **Done!** Check your home screen

## How to Install on iPhone (iOS)

1. **Open Safari** (must use Safari, not Chrome)
2. **Visit:** https://sales-app.web.app
3. **Tap the Share button** (square with arrow pointing up)
4. **Scroll down** and tap "Add to Home Screen"
5. **Name it** "Sales App"
6. **Tap "Add"**
7. **Done!** The app icon will appear on your home screen

## Using the Barcode Scanner on Mobile

Once installed, the barcode scanner will work properly because:

1. **HTTPS is enabled** (required for camera access)
2. **Camera permissions** are properly requested
3. **PWA mode** gives better hardware access

### First Time Using Scanner:

1. Open the installed app
2. Go to **Record Sale** or **Inventory** tab
3. Click the **📷 Scan** button
4. **Allow camera access** when prompted
5. Point camera at barcode
6. Scanner will automatically detect and read the barcode

## Troubleshooting

### "Add to Home Screen" option not showing

- **Android:** Make sure you're using Chrome browser
- **iPhone:** Make sure you're using Safari browser
- Try refreshing the page

### Camera not working

- Make sure you **allowed camera permissions**
- Check Settings > Apps > Sales App > Permissions > Camera
- Try closing and reopening the app

### App not installing

- Make sure you have internet connection
- Clear browser cache and try again
- Update your browser to the latest version

## Uninstalling the App

### Android:
- Long press the app icon
- Tap "Uninstall" or drag to "Remove"

### iPhone:
- Long press the app icon
- Tap "Remove App"
- Confirm "Delete App"
